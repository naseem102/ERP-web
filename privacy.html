Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ERP System (“the Application”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the ERP System desktop software and related backup features. By using the Application or this website, you also accept our Terms of Service, including the limitation of liability and exclusive-contact provisions set out there.

1. Who we are

ERP System is a Windows desktop application for business operations including company settings, financial years, customers, suppliers, inventory, purchase orders, material receipts, delivery challans, invoices, cash/bank receipts and payments, multi-bank accounts and bank book, opening balances, chart of accounts, reports, customer and supplier ledgers, and local or optional cloud backup. Contact: kingofdevilkillr1@gmail.com

2. Information we process

2.1 Business data you enter

The Application stores data you enter in a local SQLite database on your device. Depending on the modules you use, this may include:

  • Company name, address, phone, email, NTN, STRN, currency, tax %, document prefixes
  • Customer and supplier records (code, name, phone, address, city, province, NTN, opening balance)
  • Inventory items (code, name, unit, category, stock levels)
  • Purchase orders, material receipts, delivery challans (including driver name/phone), invoices (including DC reference and PO fields)
  • Receipts and payments (party, amount, cheque details, linked bank account)
  • Bank accounts (name, branch, account number, IBAN, opening balance) and bank transfers
  • Opening balances, chart of accounts, and report/ledger outputs you generate
  • PDF files you save for documents or reports

This data stays under your control on your computer unless you export it, print it, or back it up elsewhere (for example optional Google Drive backup).

2.2 Application login and license

Usernames, password hashes, and license-related values used for Application access and for unlocking company settings after the first save are stored locally for access control. We do not operate a public cloud signup system on this website. Default install credentials should be changed on first use.

2.3 Optional Google account (Google Drive backup)

If you use Sign in with Google for optional Google Drive backup, the Application requests limited access to create or update backup files in your Drive (for example under an ERP / Backups folder). Authentication is handled by Google. OAuth tokens needed for backups you start are stored locally on your device. You can disconnect Google inside the Application to delete the local token.

We do not use your Google account for advertising and do not read unrelated Drive files beyond what the backup feature requires.

2.4 Website

This website is informational. Hosting providers may log standard request data (IP, browser, pages visited). We do not build marketing profiles from casual visits.

3. Cookies on this website

We use a small number of cookies / local storage items so the site can respect your choices:

  • Necessary — stores your cookie consent decision so we do not ask on every visit.
  • Preferences (optional) — may remember simple UI choices on this device.
  • Analytics (optional) — reserved for anonymous usage measurement only if you accept it. At present we do not load any third-party analytics tool. No advertising cookies. No sale of data.

You can choose Accept all, Necessary only, or open Manage. To change your choice later, clear site data for this domain in your browser and reload the page.

4. How we use information

  • To run Application features you use (masters, documents, stock, banks, ledgers, reports, local storage).
  • To enforce local login and optional company-settings unlock (password + license).
  • To perform optional backups you explicitly start.
  • To respond to support or privacy emails you send.
  • To improve reliability and security of the software and website.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal or business information. Limited sharing may occur with:

  • Google — only if you connect Drive, under Google’s terms and scopes you approve.
  • Hosting providers — as needed to operate this website.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. Retention

Local application data remains on your device until you delete it or replace the database. Google tokens remain until you disconnect. Drive backup files remain until you delete them in Drive. Website consent preferences remain in your browser until you clear them.

7. Security

The Application is local-first and requests cloud access only for optional backup. Company settings can be locked after first save. Protect your device with OS updates and strong passwords. You are responsible for controlling who can log in on shared PCs.

8. Your choices

  • Use the Application without connecting Google Drive.
  • Disconnect Google from the Backup screen to remove local tokens.
  • Delete backup files from your own Google Drive.
  • Change the admin password and keep your license key private.
  • Control website cookies via the consent banner.
  • Email privacy questions to the address below.
