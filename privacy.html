Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ERP System (“the Application”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the ERP System desktop software and related backup features.

1. Who we are

ERP System is a Windows desktop application for business operations such as invoices, delivery challans, material receipts, payments, reports, and backups. Contact: kingofdevilkillr@gmail.com

2. Information we process

2.1 Business data you enter

The Application stores data you enter (for example parties, items, documents, and payments) in a local database on your device. This data stays under your control on that computer unless you choose to export or back it up elsewhere.

2.2 Account credentials for the Application

Login usernames and passwords for the Application are stored locally as part of the Application’s own access control. We do not operate a public cloud account system for general end-user registration on this website.

2.3 Optional Google account (Google Drive backup)

If you use Sign in with Google for optional Google Drive backup, the Application requests limited access so it can create or update backup files in your Google Drive (for example under an application folder such as ERP / Backups). Google authentication is handled by Google. We receive OAuth tokens needed to perform the backup actions you start. Tokens are stored locally on your device so you do not have to sign in every time. You can disconnect Google from inside the Application, which deletes the local token.

We do not use your Google account for advertising. We do not read unrelated files in your Drive beyond what is required for the backup feature you initiate.

2.4 Website

This website is informational. It may receive standard server or hosting logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) from the hosting provider. We do not intentionally collect personal profiles from casual website visitors.

3. How we use information

  • To run the Application features you use (documents, reports, local storage).
  • To perform optional backups you explicitly start (local folder or Google Drive).
  • To respond to support or privacy requests you send by email.
  • To improve reliability and security of the software.

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Where data protection laws apply, we process information based on performance of a contract (providing the software features you use), legitimate interests (security and product improvement), and consent (optional Google Drive connection).

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited sharing may occur with:

  • Google — only if you connect Google Drive, under Google’s terms and the scopes you approve.
  • Service providers — such as website hosting, only as needed to operate this site.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. Data retention

Local business data remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the Application, or restore/replace the database. Google OAuth tokens remain until you disconnect or remove them. Backup files in Google Drive remain in your Drive until you delete them.

7. Security

We design the Application to keep data local by default and to request cloud access only when you choose optional backup. No method of storage is 100% secure; protect your device with OS updates and account passwords.

8. Your choices

  • Use the Application without connecting Google Drive.
  • Disconnect Google Drive from the Backup screen to remove local tokens.
  • Delete backup files from Google Drive in your own Drive account.
  • Request support or privacy help at the email above.

9. Children

The Application is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

10. International users

The software may be used in different countries. Optional Google services may process data according to Google’s infrastructure and policies.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Application after updates means you accept the revised policy where permitted by law.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: kingofdevilkillr@gmail.com
